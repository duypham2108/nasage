# External replication of age-associated epithelial programmes (Crownlands Gateway atlas)

Status: **LOCKED** by the commit that adds this file. Later changes are dated deviations in the
results file, never edits here.

## 0. Purpose and what this can claim

Figures 1-4 of the manuscript are all derived from one cohort. This plan specifies the
**independent replication** of the age-associated module gradients in a second, larger cohort,
and nothing else. It is the analysis behind Figure 5b.

| Can claim | Cannot claim |
|---|---|
| "The age gradient of programme X in cell type Y reproduces in an independent cohort of 192 donors aged 20-90" | Anything about donors under 18: this cohort has none |
| "The direction and rank order of age effects agree / disagree across cohorts" | That absolute module scores are comparable between cohorts - the platforms differ |
| "Programme X is not reproducible" | That a non-replicating effect is false; power and platform are confounded with cohort |
| Age gradients within each cohort | Any vaccine outcome. No vaccine or infection endpoint exists in this cohort |

## 1. Dataset

CELLxGENE collection `8b35aa1f-6bcf-4a51-abc3-a3f336a44ae6`, "Gateway atlas of living human
olfactory epithelium" (Crownlands; DOI 10.64898/2026.06.10.731272), file
`1ff1c9c1-c51a-41ad-a980-f6502ecbe949.h5ad`, 27.65 GB, downloaded 2026-11-24 to
`D:\research\nasage_validation\data\crownlands\gateway4m.h5ad`.

4,028,275 cells, 202 donors. **Analysis set: the 192 donors with `disease == "normal"`.** The 5
Alzheimer's and 5 Parkinson's donors are excluded and not analysed in any form.

Healthy donor age distribution: 18-39: 39, 40-64: 58, 65+: 90. Ages 20-90. Median 15,273 cells
per donor. Assay: 10x Flex (probe-based), 18,127 genes.

**This dataset is not Yoshida et al. 2022 and contains none of its donors.** It is newly
generated by a single centre and is not an integrated atlas.

### 1.1 Declared weaknesses of this cohort

Recorded now so they appear in the manuscript regardless of the result.

1. **Preprint from a commercial entity**, not peer reviewed.
2. **Olfactory cleft sampling**, not inferior turbinate brushing. The respiratory cell types
   overlap but the anatomical site differs. Olfactory-specific populations are excluded (§2).
3. **Probe-based assay.** Scores are not comparable to 10x 3' data on any absolute scale. Only
   **within-cohort age gradients** are compared across cohorts (§5).
4. **No one under 18.** This cohort cannot replicate anything about childhood, which is where
   Figure 4's interferon result lives.

### 3.1 Measurability gate, applied before testing

For each module and each primary cell type, compute the fraction of cells with non-zero counts
per gene (the procedure of `validation/check_module_measurability.py`). **A module is tested in
a cell type only if at least 60% of its available genes reach 5% detection in that cell type.**
Modules failing the gate are reported as `poorly_measurable` with their detection table and are
**not** tested, not rescued by gene substitution, and not counted in the multiplicity correction.

This gate is declared now because M12 has already failed an equivalent check in 4 of 4
single-cell datasets (`results/own_cohort/MEASURABILITY.md`); it is expected to fail here too,
and the decision rule must not be chosen after seeing whether it does.

## 4. Cell subsampling, fixed before analysis

4 million cells do not fit in the available 40 GB of RAM at scoring time.

- For each donor x primary cell type, draw at most **500 cells** without replacement,
  `numpy.random.default_rng(0)`.
- A donor contributes to a cell type only if it has **at least 20 cells** of that type; donors
  below that are dropped for that cell type and the per-cell-type donor counts are reported.
- The subsample is drawn once and saved, so every analysis below uses the same cells.
- **Sensitivity:** the whole primary analysis is repeated with seed 1 and with a 1,000-cell cap.
  If any primary verdict changes, every affected module is reported as `subsample_dependent` and
  carries no claim.

## 5. Primary analysis

Unit of analysis is the **donor-level mean module score within a cell type**, as elsewhere in
this project: UCell on raw counts per cell, then averaged per donor per cell type.

- **Model:** `score ~ age_bin + sex + log10(median counts per cell) + subtype fractions`,
  fitted per cell type, exactly as `nas.tl.age_effects` defines it. `age_bin` has three levels,
  18-39 (reference), 40-64, 65+.
- **Direction: none predicted. All tests are two-sided.** Whether a programme rises or falls
  with age is the question; the gene sets were fixed long before this cohort was found, and that
  is what removes circularity.
- **Multiplicity:** BH within the primary family = (modules passing the gate) x (3 primary cell
  types). The family size is therefore not known until the gate runs; it is reported before the
  p-values are, and the smallest attainable BH is stated with it. **If the smallest attainable
  BH exceeds 0.05 the plan is reported as unattainable and no verdict is issued** - the error
  that sank `setA_derivation` and `module_confirmation` is not repeated.
- **Sensitivity:** continuous age in place of `age_bin`, same model. Reported alongside.

### 5.1 Specificity controls

- **Expression-matched random gene sets:** for each tested module and cell type, 500 random sets
  matched on mean expression in 25 bins; the empirical p is the fraction of random sets with an
  age effect at least as extreme. 500 sets puts the floor at p = 0.002.
- **Permutation null:** age bin labels permuted across donors, 500 permutations, per cell type.
- A module whose nominal effect does not clear both controls is reported as
  `not_specific` and carries no claim.

## 6. The replication readout (Figure 5b)

This is the question the cohort was brought in to answer.

- For every (module, primary cell type) tested in **both** cohorts, take the age-effect estimate
  for the **65+ versus young-adult contrast**. In our cohort that is `Eldly` versus `Adult`; in
  Crownlands it is `65+` versus `18-39`. Our cohort's `Adult` bin is 6 donors and its `Eldly`
  bin is 5, so this contrast is weak on our side and that is stated with the figure.
- **Primary readout:** Spearman correlation of the effect estimates across cohorts, with a
  permutation p. Pre-declared as the headline number for Figure 5b.
- **Secondary readout:** the sign-concordance rate, and a per-module table of both estimates with
  confidence intervals.
- Effects are **never** pooled or meta-analysed across cohorts: the platforms differ, so pooling
  would mix a platform effect into an age effect.
- **Replication is concordance of direction and relative magnitude, not joint significance.**
  A module significant in Crownlands and not in our 11-donor adult/elderly comparison is
  reported as concordant-if-the-signs-agree, with our cohort's power stated.

## 7. Failure conditions, stated in advance

| Condition | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Smallest attainable BH > 0.05 after the gate | no verdict; reported as unattainable |
| Cross-cohort Spearman is near zero or negative | **reported as a failure to replicate.** Figure 5b shows it anyway, and the manuscript states that the age gradients are cohort- or platform-specific |
| A module is `poorly_measurable` here but was tested in our cohort | reported; the cross-cohort comparison simply omits it |
| A verdict changes under seed 1 or a 1,000-cell cap | `subsample_dependent`; no claim |
| A module fails the random-set or permutation control | `not_specific`; no claim |
| Olfactory contamination suspected (e.g. a module's effect is carried by cells adjacent to olfactory populations) | reported; no post-hoc exclusion beyond the fixed list in §2 |
